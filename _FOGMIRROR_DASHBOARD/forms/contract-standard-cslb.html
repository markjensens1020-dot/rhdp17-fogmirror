Contract Standard (CSLB) — every contract must meet this

Richard Haro Drafting and Planning, Inc. · License #704179 · per CSLB Advisory (BPC 7159 & 7159.5), 06-13-2026

We use 2–3 contract types (a preliminary proposal and the working contract). The actual signed templates live as attachments in the customer email threads (e.g. Castro) and in SharePoint. A copy of the signed contract goes in each customer's 00_Intake_and_Contract folder.

DEPOSIT LIMIT (BPC 7159.5(a)(3)): contracts $10,000+ → max deposit $1,000; contracts under $10,000 → max deposit 10% of total. Applies to any upfront ask before work — down payment, retainer, or initial invoice. Revised figures go to Richard & Irma before sending.

On every document

  • The Contractor's License # (704179) appears on every Proposal/Estimate, Contract, and Invoice/Change Order.

Contract requirements (BPC 7159)

  • Written agreement, signed by contractor and homeowner before any work begins (including change/extra-work orders).
  • Basic disclosures: contractor name, business address, license #, date signed.
  • Schedule of progress payments matching work actually performed / materials delivered — no collecting ahead of the work (except the allowed down payment).
  • Notice of Cancellation section near the signature lines.
  • Format: text ≥ 10-point; major headings 10-point boldface. State the total cost in dollars and cents.

Reference

  • CSLB Sample Home Improvement Contract — cslb.ca.gov → Guides & Publications 2025.
