City & County Portals — submit & resubmit

Tap a jurisdiction to open its real E-plan portal, see the file rules, the resubmittal steps, and the published review time. Specs change — always confirm on the city's page (sources linked).

General E-plan submittal best practices

  1. PDF, vector/CAD-exported beats scans. Where scans are allowed they're for supporting docs only (150–300 dpi, OCR'd).
  2. Flatten layers & keep PDFs unsecured — reviewers must stamp, mark up and insert corrected pages (Palo Alto, Cupertino, Durham).
  3. Sheet packaging differs by city — one-sheet-per-file (Cupertino), one combined bookmarked PDF (Campbell, Gilroy, Holly Springs), or separated by trade (Durham, Palo Alto). Never reuse one city's export setting for another.
  4. Follow the exact file-naming convention the city publishes (Cupertino ≤25 chars no dashes; Palo Alto C#_Address_FILETYPE; Holly Springs ProjectName_Revision_ReviewNumber_Date).
  5. Mind file-size caps where posted: Palo Alto ~400 MB/file, Durham 200 MB/file (hard reject), Fremont 500-page Scout / 1,000-page Accela.
  6. Resubmittal = upload only the changed sheets + a written comment-response letter itemizing each comment, the fix, and the location ("See Plan" is not accepted at Palo Alto).
  7. Bubble/cloud the changes on resubmittals (Chapel Hill, Palo Alto).
  8. Signed/stamped sheets required; digital signatures generally accepted (stamps from Cycle 2 at Palo Alto).
  9. Completeness-first review is now common in CA (Jan 2024 law) — submit a truly complete package or the clock doesn't start.
  10. Plan-review fees are often due before routing (Raleigh, Fremont, Durham, Holly Springs).
Heads-up on changes: Apex is moving to a new "MyServices" portal (~fall 2026) — verify which system is live before you submit. Morgan Hill takes submittals by email (its eTRAKiT is status/parcel only). Durham City & County share one combined Building & Safety department. Many CA cities bury exact size/sheet specs inside a linked "Electronic Plan Submittal Guide" PDF — open it for the numbers.
