Client Resource Guide

Four simple tools to start your project.

We've put together a short set of free tools that help us move faster and keep you informed at every step. Each one takes just a few minutes to set up — no technical experience needed.

▦

Tool 01

Measure Your Home

Polycam — free iPhone app (LiDAR-powered)

Why it helps you: Your phone's camera can capture accurate room dimensions in minutes, which means we can begin drawing real plans right away — without scheduling a long measuring visit first.

How to use it

  1. 1 Download Polycam free from the App Store.
  2. 2 Walk each room slowly, pointing the phone at the walls, floor, and ceiling as you go.
  3. 3 Tap Share inside the app and copy the link it generates.
  4. 4 Send that link to RHDP17 — we'll take it from there.

This gets us about 95% of the way there. Our drafters field-verify the critical dimensions before anything goes to permit.

Tool 02

Snap & Send Photos

RHDP17 easy upload — no app needed

Why it helps you: Send us photos of your space, inspiration images, or paper documents in seconds — straight from your phone's camera or gallery, without downloading anything.

How to use it

  1. 1 Tap the button below to open the upload page.
  2. 2 Take photos right there or pick existing ones from your gallery. Works great as a scanner for paper documents too.
  3. 3 Add your name so we know who sent it.
  4. 4 Hit Send. We'll have it instantly.

A free browser tool for saving design ideas from anywhere on the web

How to use it

Smart Financing

Plan around the right loan — and save.

Many homeowners save money by planning their project around a renovation or investment loan rather than a construction loan. The differences in terms and timing can be significant. We'll help you design to your budget so you're working from solid numbers when you sit down with your bank.

RHDP17 is not a lender or financial advisor. Please confirm all financing details with your lending institution.
