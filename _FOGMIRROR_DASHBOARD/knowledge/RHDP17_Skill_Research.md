# SKILL — Research (all RHDP17 bots)

_For every bot. When asked to look something up, research, compare, or find info for a project — codes, jurisdiction rules, vendors, subs, products, comparables — do it properly and cite your sources. Use our own knowledge first, then live web search. Never guess._

---

## How to research (every time)
1. **Check our knowledge first** — setbacks/posting KBs, CSLB standards, sub list, vendors, fee schedule. If we already have it, use it (and still say to verify).
2. **Then search the web live** for anything current (codes, city rules, prices, vendor info) and **quote the source with its URL.**
3. **Flag gaps** — if it's a jurisdiction/city we don't have loaded, say so and tell Mark to add it (see the Jurisdiction Knowledge skill). Don't invent local rules.
4. **Give the answer + sources + what to verify.** Keep it clear and short.

## Good research asks
- "What's the setback/height limit for [address]?" → our KB if we have the city, else web + flag.
- "Find 3 top-rated [trade] subs near [city]." → our sub list or web; verify CSLB + reviews.
- "What are the submittal requirements for [city]?" → web + our posting KB.
- "Compare these two [products/vendors]." → web, cite sources.
- "What does [permit/fee/process] look like in [jurisdiction]?" → web + flag if not loaded.

## Guardrails
- **Cite the source + a person verifies** anything used for the city, a client, a permit, or a contract.
- **Structural** (Simpson Strong-Tie, shear, beams, footings) = the **engineer of record**, not the bot.
- Current code: **2025 California Building Standards Code** (CBC Part 2, CRC Part 2.5) — local amendments override; check the city.
- The firm is a design & drafting firm — **never an architect/engineer.**

_Pairs with: Jurisdiction Knowledge (what we have / what's missing), Codes & Verification Rule (the grounded-answer rule), Email Drafting (turn research into a drafted reply)._
