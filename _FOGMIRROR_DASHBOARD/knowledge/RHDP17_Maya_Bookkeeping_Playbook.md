
# RHDP17 — Maya's Bookkeeping Playbook

_Maya's brain: how billing and collections run, start to finish. Load into the Maya bot. Pairs with `RHDP17_Billing_With_Maya.md` (the team flow) — this is the deeper how-to. Not legal/tax advice; confirm accounting steps with your CPA._

---

## 1. The fee schedule (design services agreement)
Bill each project in three milestones off the total contract:

| Milestone | Trigger | % | On a $10,000 job |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Retainer** | At booking | 25% | $2,500 |
| **Working Drawings** | Design accepted / start of working drawings | 50% | $5,000 |
| **Permit** | At permit | 25% | $2,500 |

_Design-services agreement = separate professional-services fee, so the home-improvement deposit cap doesn't apply. If it's ever a home-improvement contract instead, the $1,000-or-10% up-front deposit cap applies — check `RHDP17_Contract_Standards_CSLB.md`._

## 2. Make an invoice (each milestone)
1. Trigger hits (booking / design accepted / permit).
2. Draft it — ask Fog Mirror: "Create an invoice for [client] for [amount] — [milestone]," or use the invoice templates in the Customers folder.
3. Fill: invoice #, date, client address/email, project, the milestone line, amount, payment method.
4. **Number invoices consistently:** `INV-YYYY-MMDD-#` (e.g., INV-2026-0716-1 retainer, -2 working drawings).
5. Get the OK (Richard/Irma) if it's unusual, then send.

## 3. Send & collect
- Email the invoice (PDF) to the client; log the send date.
- **Collections cadence:** Day 0 send → **Day 3** friendly nudge → **Day 7** reminder + call → **Day 14** firm follow-up (loop in Richard/Irma). Ask Fog Mirror: "Draft a payment reminder to [client] for their [milestone] invoice."
- Accept the client's payment method (check payable to *Richard Haro Drafting & Planning, Inc.*, or card via the card-auth form / your processor).
- **Never start work on a milestone before its payment clears** unless Richard approves.

## 4. Record & track
- Mark each invoice **Sent → Paid** with the date and method.
- Keep a running **balance per client** (what's billed, paid, outstanding).
- File the paid invoice in the client's `02_CONSTRUCTION / Invoices_and_Billing` (or `00_Intake_and_Contract` for the retainer).
- Ask Fog Mirror anytime: "Who has an outstanding balance?" / "What has [client] paid and what's due?"

## 5. In your accounting software (e.g., QuickBooks)
_(Adjust to whatever RHDP uses.)_
- Create the customer once; add each invoice under them.
- Match the invoice # and milestone description to the sent PDF.
- Record the payment against the invoice when it clears; reconcile to the bank monthly.
- Run a monthly **A/R aging** report to catch overdue balances.

## 6. Month-end close (quick)
- All invoices marked Sent/Paid with dates.
- A/R aging reviewed; overdue items chased.
- Payments reconciled to the bank.
- Subcontractor bills (if any) recorded; W-9s on file for 1099s.

## 7. Guardrails (hard)
- **Maya drafts, records, and collects — but money OUT (refunds, vendor payments, transfers) = Irma or Richard only.**
- The AI never runs a charge or sends money automatically — a person does it in the payment system.
- Keep client card/bank details out of plain email/forms; use the card-auth form / your secure processor.

## Maya's phrases (type to Fog Mirror)
- "Create an invoice for [client] for [amount] — [milestone]."
- "Draft a payment reminder to [client] for their [milestone] invoice."
- "Who has an outstanding balance / what's overdue?"
- "What has [client] paid so far and what's still due?"
- "Draft a thank-you confirming we received [client]'s payment."
