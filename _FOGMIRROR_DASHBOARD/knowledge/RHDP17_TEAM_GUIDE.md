## 2. What this is (the 30-second picture)

One place to see every project, work the emails, open client files, and get help from the AI. It pulls from your Microsoft 365 (Outlook, Teams, SharePoint) and your project data. Three things happen here:
- **You see** where every project stands (color-coded by stage).
- **The AI drafts** your emails — **a person always reviews and sends.** Nothing goes out on its own.
- **You open** any client's files, portals, and tools from one screen.

## 4. The AI — who to talk to and what to say

There are **two AI parts**, and it helps to know which is which:

### A) "🎙️ Fog Mirror" — the assistant you type to
Open **Fog Mirror** on the dashboard and **type** your request in the box, then press Enter. (No need to talk to it — typing is the way.) Keep it simple: **name the client/project + the task.** See the full phrase list in **§10 — Phrases & Prompts Cheat Sheet** below. A few to start:
- "What's the status on the *Castro* project?"
- "Which projects have something waiting on me today?"
- "Draft a friendly email to *[client]* that their plans were submitted to the city."

If it doesn't know something yet, that's fine — tell Mark so we can teach it.

### B) The email drafter — runs automatically, no phrases needed
Behind the scenes, the system reads the shared **haroinc@rhdpinc.com** inbox a few times a day, sorts the mail, and **writes draft replies into Outlook Drafts** for you. You don't ask it to — it just does it. Your job is to **open Drafts, read, edit if needed, and send.**

**The golden rule: the AI writes drafts, a PERSON always sends. Nothing sends by itself.**

## 5. Emails & Drafts — the daily flow

1. Click **📝 Drafts (approve)** (or the Drafts button on any page) → opens **Outlook Drafts**.
2. You'll see AI-written replies ready to go.
3. **Read each one, fix anything, then hit Send** yourself.
4. Client-facing drafts should be reviewed by **Cassie or Irma** before sending.

That's it — the AI does the typing, you do the sending.

## 6. Where files save & SharePoint access (important)

**The master files live in SharePoint:**
`AllProjectsAlert → Shared Documents → RHDP DRAFTING PROJECTS 2025 → [Customer name]`

- The **📁 Customer files** buttons on the dashboard open that shared library, so everyone sees the same files on any device.
- Each customer folder has the standard layout: intake/contract, design (plans, emails, city, consultants, selections), and construction (subs, billing, inspections).

**To make sure everyone has access (Mark does this once):**

2. Each teammate then opens the library in the browser and clicks **"Add shortcut to OneDrive"** (or **Sync**) — now the same files also show as a normal folder on their computer.

**Customer Intake — where it goes:** on the intake form, fill it out, hit **Submit Intake Form**, and it **downloads a named file** like `Quintanilla_Sandra_Client_Intake_2026-07-16.html`. **Save that file into that client's folder** in the SharePoint library above. ## 10. Phrases & Prompts Cheat Sheet (type these to Fog Mirror)

**How to phrase it:** name the **client or project** + the **task**, in plain words, one ask at a time. Example: *"Draft a follow-up to Castro about their selections."* You don't need special commands — just say what you want.

Three kinds of requests: **Ask** (get info), **Draft** (write an email for you to review & send), **Do** (perform a task — some of these come online as automations are finished; if one isn't wired yet, Fog Mirror will tell you).

### New client & intake (Cassie leads)
- "Start a new client intake for **[Name]**."
- "Create a file for **[Name]** and load the standard contract set."
- "What info do I still need from **[client]** to open their project?"
- "Draft a welcome email to **[client]** with the intake form link."
- "Summarize the new lead from **[client]**'s email."

### Daily status & priorities (Mark & Cassie)
- "What needs my attention today?"
- "Give me the morning debrief."
- "What's the status of **[client]**'s project?"
- "Which projects are waiting on the **city / the client / us**?"
- "What's next on **[project]**?"

### Emails & follow-ups
- "Draft a friendly update to **[client]** that their plans were submitted."
- "Reply to **[client]**'s last email about **[topic]**."
- "Write a follow-up to **[client]** — we haven't heard back in a week."
- "Draft a thank-you to **[client]** for their deposit."
- _(Always review and send it yourself.)_

### Design & plan-check (Jesus)
- "What corrections did the city send on **[project]**?"
- "Draft a reply to the plan-checker on **[project]**."
- "When is **[project]**'s resubmittal due?"
- "Draft a note to the engineer on **[project]** about **[issue]**."

### Selections (Cassie / Irma)
- "What selections is **[client]** still missing?"
- "Draft a reminder to **[client]** to finish their tile and cabinet picks."
- "Where is **[client]** on their selection board?"

### City / permits / zoning
- "What jurisdiction is **[project]** in and what are the submittal requirements?"
- "What's the setback and height limit for **[address]**?"
- "Do we need story poles for **[project]**?"

### Construction (Jesus / Richard)
- "What inspections are coming up on **[project]**?"
- "Draft a note to the subcontractor on **[project]** about **[issue]**."
- "What's outstanding on **[project]**'s punch list?"

### Billing & money (Maya — with Richard/Irma approval)
- "Create an invoice for **[client]** for **[amount]**."
- "Who has an outstanding balance?"
- "Draft a payment reminder to **[client]**."
- _(Money out = Irma or Richard only.)_

### Files & how-to
- "Open **[client]**'s files."
- "How do I **[do X]** in the dashboard?"
