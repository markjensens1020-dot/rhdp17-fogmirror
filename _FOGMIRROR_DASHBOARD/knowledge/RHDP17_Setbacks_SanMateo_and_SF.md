# Residential Setback & Buildability Knowledge Base — San Mateo County + San Francisco
### RHDP17 drafting & permitting quick-reference · compiled July 2026 · FIELD-VERIFY every number against current code + the specific parcel before drawing. Not legal advice.

## Statewide ADU baseline (applies EVERYWHERE)
- **4 ft** side & rear minimum — a city cannot require more for a compliant ADU up to 800 sf.
- Must allow at least an **800 sf ADU** even if it fails local coverage/FAR/front-setback.
- Detached ADU height: **16 ft** base; **18 ft** within ½ mile of major transit; +2 ft if roof pitch matches the house.
- Garage/structure conversions: no added setback beyond existing; no replacement parking (SB 1211, Jan 2025).
- Source: HCD ADU Handbook; ABAG ADU State Laws Summary (7/2025).

## City of San Mateo — zone R1-B / R1-C (primary; R1-A = large-lot)
- Front 15 ft (garage 20 ft) · Side interior 5 ft · Side street 15% lot width (7.5–15 ft; 2nd story ≥10 ft; garage 20 ft) · Rear 15 ft (**25 ft above single story**) · FAR 0.5 (R1-A: 0.4 +0.2 over 10k sf) · min 6,000 sf (B)/5,000 (C). Height via daylight plane §27.18.050 (verify).
- Source: SMMC Ch. 27.18; R-1 Development Standards FAQ.

## Redwood City — zone R-1
- Front 15 ft · Side interior 6 ft OR ½ building-face height, whichever greater (lots <60 ft: 10% width, min 4 ft) · Side street garage 20 ft (verify general) · Rear 20 ft · Height 28 ft / ≤2.5 stories · Coverage 40% · **FAR: verify current cap (FAR urgency ordinance).**
- Source: Redwood City Zoning Code (Municode); R-district SFR handout; FAR Urgency Ordinance.

## Menlo Park — zone R-1-U (urban; R-1-S = suburban)
- Front 25% of lot depth (10–25 ft) · Side interior 10% lot width (5–10 ft) [R-1-S flat 10 ft] · Side street ≥12 ft · Rear: **verify** (daylight plane + FAL govern) · Height 28 ft (<20k sf) / 30 ft (≥20k) · Coverage 40%→35% sliding · **Floor Area Limit** 2,800 sf for 5–7k lots +25% over 7k (R-1-S: 2nd floor ≤50% of FAL) · Daylight plane Ch. 16.67.
- Source: MPMC Ch. 16.16 / 16.14; Zoning Summary Sheet 2025.

## Atherton — zone R-1A (1-acre min; whole town large-lot)
- Front **60 ft** · Side interior **50 ft** each · Side street verify · Rear **60 ft** · Height 30 ft (to 34 ft only via Special Structure Permit AND +5 ft yard per 1 ft height) · Floor area 18% of lot (≥1 ac); sub-acre = lot×0.163 + 726 sf (min 2,250) · min 1 acre.
- **Tree-protection warning signs mandatory on every fence.** ADU state 4 ft still applies (large base setbacks don't override it).
- Source: AMC Ch. 17.32 / §17.32.040 / §17.54.030; Ch. 17.52 ADUs.

## East Palo Alto — zone R-LD (low-density = SFR)
- Front 10 ft to living / **23 ft to garage** (corner vision 12 ft) · Side interior 5 ft · Side street 5 ft · Rear: **min 750 sf usable rear-yard** (verify dimensional min) · Height 2 stories or 26 ft · Coverage 50% · FAR 0.55 · min 5,000 sf/50 ft.
- Source: EPA Title 18 Development Code, Article 2.

## Unincorporated San Mateo County — zone R-1 ⚠️ CODE IN TRANSITION
- **No single county R-1 setback.** R-1 is a *use* district; dimensions come from the mapped **"S" combining district** (e.g., R-1/S-101), each with its own setback/height/lot table. **Pull the parcel's exact R-1/S combo before drawing.**
- County **repealed old zoning (Div VI) → adopted new Title 8 Development Code Oct 8 2024.** Confirm which code version governs the parcel. ADU state 4 ft; County ADU Article 6.
- Source: County R-1 District Regulations page; Get Zoning Information; Zoning Regulations index.

## City & County of San Francisco — zone RH-1 (one-family) — NOT simple numbers
- **Front:** averaging — match average of the two adjacent buildings / block-face (§132); no fixed min.
- **Side yards:** generally none required in RH-1 (lot-line to lot-line); verify mapped exceptions.
- **Rear yard:** RH-1 = **30% of lot depth, min 15 ft** (applications on/after Jan 15 2019, §134). Other RH (e.g., RH-2) = 45%. Trickiest number — confirm which applies.
- **Height:** from the **mapped height/bulk district** (commonly **40 ft**) — read the zoning map, not the RH letter. FAR/coverage governed by rear-yard + open-space (verify).
- ADU: state 4 ft + SF ADU overlays (§207) — verify.
- Source: SF Planning Code §209.1 / §134 / §132; ZA Bulletin No. 5; Residential Districts Controls Summary.

## Trickiest — never quote a flat number without checking the parcel
- **San Francisco** (front averaged, rear = % of depth, side none, height off the map).
- **Atherton** (60/50/60 on 1 acre; height buy-back; sub-acre floor-area formula).
- **Menlo Park** (everything is a formula + daylight plane + FAL).
- **Unincorporated San Mateo County** (R-1/S combo + Oct-2024 Title 8 transition).
- Quotable base numbers (verify FAR/coverage which move): San Mateo City, Redwood City, Atherton, East Palo Alto.
