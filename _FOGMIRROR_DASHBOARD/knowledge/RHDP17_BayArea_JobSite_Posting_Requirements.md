# RHDP17 — Bay Area Job-Site Posting Requirements (by jurisdiction)
### What must be posted AT a residential project site. Researched July 6 2026 · verify per project; not legal advice.

**Baseline everywhere (California Building Code):** CBC §105.7 — the **permit/job card** must be posted/kept on site; CBC §107.5 / CRC R106.3.1 — one set of **approved, stamped plans** kept on site for the inspector. Every jurisdiction below has this as the floor. The table shows what each city ADDS on top.

## Notable differences (more than card + plans)
- **Story poles** (physical silhouette for design-review hearings): **Los Altos Hills, Monte Sereno, Saratoga, Los Gatos** (2nd-story trigger), **Palo Alto** (staff discretion). Certified by surveyor/engineer; owner may not self-certify (Hills/Monte Sereno).
- **Construction-hours / address placards:** **Palo Alto** (5' street-visible), **Mountain View** (prominent), **Campbell** (jobsite-signage form).
- **Wage-rights daily notice:** **Sunnyvale** (15,000+ sq ft).
- **Tree-protection signage:** **Atherton** (on every protection fence).
- **Strictest card rule:** **Redwood City** (weatherproof sleeve, street-visible).
- **SF unique:** §106A.4.6 permit-issuance sign (dwelling removal), §311 neighbor-notice board, AB-032 plans-or-stop.
- **County extra (unincorp. Santa Clara):** setback certification at 1st foundation inspection + Fire water-source approval before foundation (combustible construction).

## Verify before relying (not confirmed in a city-specific source; rest on CBC baseline)
Monte Sereno, Los Gatos, Saratoga, Morgan Hill (posting wording); visible-posting specifics for Palo Alto, Atherton, East Palo Alto, Menlo Park. No mandatory stormwater/BMP *placard* was confirmed anywhere (BMP compliance still required). **Morgan Hill** — call the building dept. **Los Altos ≠ Los Altos Hills** (only the Hills has story poles).
