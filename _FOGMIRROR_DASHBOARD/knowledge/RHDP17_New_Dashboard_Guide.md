# RHDP17 Dashboard — How It Works (New Clean Version)
### For Cassie and the whole team

_The dashboard is now one clean page: pick a customer, and every process for them is stacked and color-coded, top to bottom. No more wall of buttons._

---

## Getting in
1. Open Chrome → **`rhdp17-fogmirror.markjensens1020.workers.dev/?v=4`**
2. **Sign in with Microsoft** — your **@rhdpinc.com** account.
3. It lands you straight on the **Overview** (the stacked view). If it ever looks old, press **Ctrl + Shift + R**.

---

## What you see
**Customer name, big, on top.** Type a customer's name in the box — their name shows large, and everything below is theirs.

**Their processes, stacked in order, each its own color:**
1. 🟢 **Design** (green)
2. 🟥 **Construction** (burgundy)
3. 🔵 **Subcontractors · Bidding** (blue)
4. 🟡 **Selection Board** (gold)
5. 🟣 **Bookkeeping** (violet)

**Each process is a big status bar.** The bar is split into that process's steps:
- **Bright segment = done** (✓)
- **White-outlined segment = the step they're on now** (●)
- **Dark segment = not there yet**

So at a glance you see exactly where every process stands for that customer.

**Hover any step → the tools pop up.** Rest your mouse on a segment and it shows what you need for that step — the form, the sub list, the selection board, the inspection, etc., with links where it makes sense. (On a tablet, tap and hold.)

**Click a process name (↗)** to open its full working board.

---

## The 3 buttons up top
- 📝 **Drafts** — opens Outlook Drafts, where the AI-written emails wait for a person to review and **send**.
- 📋 **Forms** — one button, opens the Forms hub with every form (intake, onboarding, selection board, contracts, retainer, card auth). They're also pre-loaded in each customer's folder.
- 🎙️ **Fog Mirror** — the AI assistant. **Type or talk** to it. Ask about a project, draft an email, look up a code, find a form. It answers from our own playbooks and cites its sources.

---

## Money lives in one place
