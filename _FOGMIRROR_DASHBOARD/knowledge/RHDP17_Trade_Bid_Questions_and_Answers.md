## Answers every sub asks (all trades)
- **What's the project / where?** New 2-story, 800 sf attached ADU + alteration to the existing home, 1640 Gran Teton Dr, Milpitas (Santa Clara County). Plans attached.
- **What are the plans / codes?** 2025 CRC/CBC/CMC/CPC/CEC/Energy/CalGreen + 2026 Milpitas Municipal Code. Type V-B, 2 stories, R-3/U, **no fire sprinkler.**

## Foundation / Concrete
- **Slab-on-grade or raised? Footing sizes?** → Per Structural Sheet **S1**.
- **Soils/geotech report or special inspection?** → Structural is the **engineer of record**; special inspection/testing is owner-retained. Coordinate, don't bid it.
- **Site access / existing conditions?** → Existing single-family lot, ADU at the rear; confirm access on a site walk.
- **Who pumps / who supplies concrete?** → Confirm in your bid (inclusions/exclusions).

## Framing
- **Who supplies the lumber/hardware package?** → Confirm in your bid — state if material is included or labor-only.
- **Engineered members, shear, hold-downs per plan?** → Per Structural **S2** (and any structural details/calcs).
- **Tie-in to the existing house?** → Yes, framing includes tie-in to the existing dwelling.

## Plumbing
- **Fixture count and locations?** → ADU kitchen + bath per the floor plans (rough + finish).
- **Water heater — tankless / gas / electric / heat-pump?** → Per plans/Title 24; **confirm the selected unit** with the office.
- **New sewer lateral or tie to existing? Water service?** → Confirm from the site/civil plan; if not shown, get it from Richard.
- **Gas line needed?** → Only if gas appliances are selected — confirm.

## Electrical
- **New panel/subpanel and service size?** → Per Sheets **SHT-6 / SHT-7**.
- **Title 24 / CalGreen extras (EV-ready, solar-ready)?** → Per the energy/CalGreen sheets — confirm which apply.
- **Who supplies fixtures?** → Typically **owner-supplied fixtures, sub installs** — confirm per the selections checklist.

## HVAC / Mechanical
- **System type — mini-split, central, heat pump?** → Per plans + Title 24 (heat-pump/high-efficiency likely for energy compliance); confirm the specified equipment.
- **Is the Title 24 load calc / equipment spec provided?** → Yes, **T24 is in the plan set** — bid to it.
- **Ducted or ductless? Ventilation (CALGreen IAQ)?** → Per plans; confirm.

## Insulation
- **Required R-values and assemblies?** → Per the **Title 24 energy compliance** sheet.
- **Batt, blown, or spray? Air sealing included?** → Confirm type per T24; include air sealing.

## Drywall
- **Finish level and texture type?** → Confirm (smooth / orange-peel / match existing) with the office.
- **Ceilings — texture or smooth?** → Confirm.
- **Hang + tape + texture, ready for paint?** → Yes.

## Stucco / Siding
- **Finish, color, texture?** → Per elevations + client selection.
- **1-coat or 3-coat system?** → Confirm; **lath inspection is required before stucco.**

## Windows / Doors
- **Who orders — sub or owner?** → Confirm; **VELUX skylight supply is included** in this scope.
- **Title 24 U-factor / SHGC, egress?** → Per the energy sheet and floor plans.
- **Color / grid pattern?** → Client selection (see selections checklist).

## Flooring
- **Material and who supplies?** → Per client selection; **confirm square footage from the plans.**
- **Subfloor prep / leveling included?** → Confirm.

## Cabinets / Millwork
- **Who designs/supplies? Kitchen + bath?** → Per client selection; ADU kitchen and bath.
- **Countertops in this scope?** → Usually a **separate fabricator** (templated after cabinets set) — coordinate, confirm.

## Painting
- **Colors and number of coats?** → Per client selection.
- **Interior and exterior both?** → Yes — prep, prime, finish.
