# RHDP17 — How to Run Billing & Collections (Maya, Bookkeeper)

_For Cassie, Irma, Mark, Jesus, Richard. How a milestone becomes an invoice, gets sent, and gets collected — cleanly, with the right approvals._

---

## The fee schedule (design services agreement)
Every design project bills in three milestones off the total:

| Milestone | When | % | Example on $10,000 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Retainer** | At booking | 25% | **$2,500** |
| **Working Drawings** | Design accepted / start of working drawings | 50% | **$5,000** |
| **Permit** | At permit | 25% | **$2,500** |

_(This is a design-services agreement — a separate professional-services fee — so the home-improvement deposit cap doesn't apply. Not legal advice.)_

---

## The flow — milestone → invoice → sent → collected

1. **A milestone is hit** (client books, accepts design, or permit issues). Whoever sees it (usually Cassie or Mark) flags it.
2. **Request the invoice.** In Fog Mirror, type:
   > "Create an invoice for **[client]** for **[amount]** — **[milestone]** (25% retainer / 50% working drawings / 25% permit)."
   It drafts the invoice. Or hand the details straight to **Maya**.
3. **Maya finalizes & sends.** Maya (bookkeeper) checks the amounts, fills in the client's details and payment method, and emails the invoice to the client.
4. **Collect.** Cassie or Maya follows up (email/call/text) to collect. Payment runs through your payment system — **done by Maya**, not the AI.
5. **Record it.** Maya marks it paid and updates the client's balance.

---

## Who does what

- **Cassie** — flags the milestone, requests the invoice, emails/calls the client, and follows up to collect. Routes it to Maya.
- **Maya (bookkeeper)** — finalizes and sends the invoice, runs/records the payment, tracks balances and collections.
- **Irma / Richard** — **money approval.** Any payment out (refund, vendor payment) and any unusual charge gets their OK first.
- **Mark / Jesus / Richard** — do the design work; tell Cassie the moment a milestone is reached so the invoice goes out same-day.

**Rule: money OUT (refunds/payments) = Irma or Richard only. The AI never charges or sends money — a person does.**

---

## Billing phrases to type into Fog Mirror

- "Create an invoice for **[client]** for **[amount]** — retainer (25%)."
- "Create the working-drawings invoice for **[client]** — 50%."
- "Draft a payment reminder to **[client]** for their **[retainer / working-drawings]** invoice."
- "Who has an outstanding balance?"
- "What has **[client]** paid so far and what's still due?"
- "Draft a thank-you to **[client]** confirming we received their payment."
