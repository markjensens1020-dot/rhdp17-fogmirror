# RHDP17 — Subcontractor Bid Invitation Template (reusable)

_Cassie: copy this for each sub. Fill the [brackets]. Send the **same scope and due date to all 3 subs** for a trade so the bids compare apples-to-apples. Or type to Fog Mirror: "Draft a bid invitation to [sub] for [trade] on [job], due [date]."_

---

## Email version

**Subject:** Bid Request — [Trade] — [Project Name], [City]

Hi [Sub company / contact name],

Richard Haro Drafting & Planning is requesting a bid for the **[trade]** scope on **[project name]**, located at **[project address], [city]**. We'd like to include you.

**Scope of work:** [1–2 line description of the trade scope]. Full plans are attached (sheets [__]).

**Please include in your bid:**
- Itemized price for the [trade] scope
- Inclusions and exclusions
- Estimated timeline / earliest availability
- Any assumptions or clarifications

**Bid due:** [date].

**If awarded, we'll need before starting:** active CSLB license (correct classification), a Certificate of Insurance naming **Richard Haro Drafting & Planning as additional insured**, a W-9, and workers' comp (or valid exemption). A signed subcontractor agreement follows award.

Questions? Reply here or call **[phone]**.

Thank you,
**[Your name]**
Richard Haro Drafting & Planning, Inc. · CA Lic #704179
[phone] · [email]

## Quick checklist per trade
- [ ] Sent to **3** verified subs (same scope + due date)
