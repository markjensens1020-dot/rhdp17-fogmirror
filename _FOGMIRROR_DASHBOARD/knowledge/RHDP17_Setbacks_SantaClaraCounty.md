**ADU baseline (all jurisdictions):** California state law = **4 ft side & rear**, at least one detached ADU up to 800 sf ministerial, min 16 ft height (up to 18 ft near transit / 25 ft if attached). Cities cannot impose greater side/rear setbacks on a state-compliant ADU. Front-setback and larger-ADU rules can still be locally regulated. Per-city notes below.

### 6. Town of Los Altos Hills (strictest — formula-driven)
- **Primary zone:** R-A (Residential-Agricultural, 1-acre / 43,560 sf min lot). *No simple coverage %.* Ord. 621 amended R-A effective 1-08-2026.
- **Front 40 · Side/rear 30 (all "other" setbacks) · Height 27 ft** (up to 32 with extra setback + sloped roof; absolute cap 35 ft).
- **Buildability by formula (LUF = Lot Unit Factor; S = avg slope):**
  - Max Development Area (MDA): S≤10% → LUF×15,000; 10–30% → LUF×[15,000−375(S−10)]; ≥30% → LUF×7,500 (floor 7,500).
  - Max Floor Area (MFA): S≤10% → LUF×6,000; 10–30% → LUF×[6,000−50(S−10)]; ≥30% → LUF×5,000 (floor 5,000).
- **Estate homes (≥10,000 sf floor):** setbacks scale up (Front 44→60, Side/Rear 33→45).
- **ADU:** state 4 ft side & rear; verify Town ADU overlay.
- **Code:** LAHMC Title 10, Ch. 1, Art. 5 (§§10-1.501–.508).
- **Source:** https://ecode360.com/43999500 *(full text verified — highest confidence)*

## CITIES WITH UNUSUALLY STRICT / COMPLEX SETBACK SCHEMES

Flag these for extra care — a single flat number will not size a project correctly:

1. **Los Altos Hills (R-A)** — the outlier. No coverage %; buildability is governed by **LUF × slope formulas (MDA and MFA)**, 1-acre min lot, 160-ft inscribed building circle, 40 ft front / 30 ft other setbacks. Always run the Town's MFA/MDA worksheet. Ord. 621 changes effective 1/8/2026.

2. **Palo Alto (R-1)** — **daylight-plane** rules (side plane 10 ft @ 45°, rear plane 16 ft @ 60°) are the binding massing constraint, not the flat setbacks. Plus **contextual/block-average front setback**, tiered FAR (0.45+0.30), and Individual Review for two-story.

3. **Monte Sereno (R-1-8/20/44)** — **two-tier (1st/2nd story) setbacks that scale up with lot size**, plus height measured at the setback line increasing with additional setback. Never apply one flat number.

4. **Saratoga (R-1 districts)** — **two-tier setbacks + graduated allowable-floor-area schedule** keyed to net site area, slope, and height (not a flat FAR).

5. **Los Altos (R1-10)** — height drops from 27 ft to 20 ft if coverage exceeds 30%; narrow-lot two-story side-yard formula; daylight-plane overlay.

6. **Mountain View (R1)** — interior side yard is a **matrix by lot width AND area**; rear yard is a percentage of lot depth. Verify the exact cell per parcel.

### Naming / measurement gotchas
- **Morgan Hill has no "R1"** — use RE/RDL/RDM/RDH.
- **Gilroy measures street-adjacent setbacks from face of curb**, not property line.
- **"City of Santa Clara" ≠ "unincorporated Santa Clara County"** — different codes and different numbers.
- **San José R-1-5 vs R-1-8:** identical setbacks; the number is min lot size, and R-1-5's min lot is larger.

### ADU note (all jurisdictions)
State law sets the floor: **4 ft side & rear**, one detached ADU ≤800 sf ministerial, ≥16 ft height. Local size/height/front overlays vary and have been amended repeatedly through 2025 — confirm each city's current ADU chapter before finalizing ADU plans. Campbell's and Santa Clara's older local ADU setback language is superseded by state law where more permissive.
