📞 Consultation Sales Script

For Richard / Cassie / New PM
Opening

"Hi [Name], this is [your name] from Richard Haro Drafting and Planning. We received your inquiry about your [project type] at [address]. I'd love to learn more about your vision — do you have about 15 minutes?"

Discover Their Vision

"Tell me — when you walk into your ideal home after we're done, what do you see? What does it feel like? Is it open and bright? Are there walls you want gone?"

Open Concept Question

"A lot of our clients want to open things up — knock down walls between the kitchen and living room, raise ceilings, let more light in. Is that on your list? Which walls bother you most?"

Square Footage Question

"Are you looking to expand the footprint, or work with what you have? We can do both — an addition adds square footage, a remodel maximizes what's already there. What are you thinking?"

Budget Anchor

"To make sure we design something that fits your budget, can you share a rough range? Even a ballpark helps us right-size the plans. Everything we design, we can phase if needed."

Close to Next Step

"Here's what I'd like to do: send you our Design Binder form so you can tell us everything — materials, colors, appliances, layout preferences. Then Richard reviews it and we come back with a design proposal and fee estimate within 5 business days. Sound good?"
