The play · investment models we design around

Ways smart owners build value

House-Hack ADU. Live in one space, rent the other — qualify as an owner-occupant (often less down, better rate).

Add-Value Remodel / Addition. "Forced appreciation" — improve the home to raise its value on your terms, not the market's.

BRRRR. Add value → refinance → reinvest into the next property.

Lower-entry Triangle. Comparable homes here run ~70% less than the Bay Area — a friendlier place to start a portfolio.

Local support. Many NC cities, counties & the state back new owners, ADUs & property improvements — we check what's current for your address.
